Till - privacy

Till is a personal till app. It has no server and no analytics, and it does not share data with anyone.

What it stores

Sales, daily totals and quick items are stored in your browser on the device you use the till on.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect a Google account, Till asks for the drive.file permission only. This lets it create and update its own files - a "Till records" folder holding a Google Sheet for each closed day and a backup file - and nothing else in your Drive. Till cannot see or change any other file.

The sign-in token stays in your browser and is sent only to Google. You can disconnect at any time in the till's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files Till has created stay in your Drive until you delete them.
